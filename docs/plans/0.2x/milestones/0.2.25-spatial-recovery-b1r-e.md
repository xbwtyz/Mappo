## P3 — destruction and optional regeneration

Unified destruction search: building-first and whole eligible property-group retirement, block/batch/empty-shell/child restoration, same-block alternative retirement exchanges, same-set concession optimization. Optional additive regeneration with fresh IDs and unchanged surviving geometry; never reuse retired identity. Costs: gross retirements, demolished footprint, scaled footprint loss, frontage/access changes, displacement, deformation and regeneration. Prefer nonincreasing original retirement counts within authorized tier, compare concessions separately; at most three meaningful validated alternatives initially. Gates: independent 18/18, >=10 without demolition, 627/627 strict, all 84 prior demolition successes, zero unauthorized retirements/invalid accepted candidates/reused retired IDs; exact replay, ledger, history and portable reopen. Quality targets <=13 properties, <=17 plots, <=26 buildings retired across compression corpus; misses require documented comparison and review, not fabricated integrated results.

## P4 — worker and universal recovery UI

Reuse offline worker lifecycle, full expensive search and verification in worker; compact versioned deltas. Bind project/revision/request/intent/permission and candidate/ledger authorization. Inspector/Review toggles all relaxed permissions off initially; alternative frontage, uniform scale limit 5/10/20% (10% default when enabled), unified generated-development destruction, separate regeneration. Show gross retired/restored/new identities, area and exact concessions. Stop search retains verified incumbent; Continue resumes/restarts bounded optimization; Cancel discards draft/authorization; Reset Preview restores accepted geometry; new edit invalidates obsolete result; Apply independently validates exact reviewed witness and atomically records universal history. Refuse Apply if history cannot record. Offline Firefox/Chromium file://, no required network, worker errors/cancel/termination, stale rejection, parity/reconstruction, atomic history, no expensive relaxed main-thread fallback, preserve shortcuts. Targets: main-thread slices 8ms soft, P95 input <=50ms in qualification environment, progress 5–10/s, immediate stale suppression, no material strict preview regression, smaller transport than duplicated snapshots. Measure search and Apply separately; no universal latency promise.
