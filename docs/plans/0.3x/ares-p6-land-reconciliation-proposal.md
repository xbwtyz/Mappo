# ARES P6 — proposed detached land reconciliation work packages

Status: planning only. No P6 implementation authorized by this document. Preserve consolidated approved P6 direction, SAFE-01–15, AP-01–12, landscape providers, UAEF, Schema 11, portable/workspace 1, exact positions, P4/SRE fallback, budgets and explicit permission gates.

## Objective
Take a separately independently qualified road candidate and establish whether the *whole city* can survive that change. A road-only witness is not an accepted city, and P6 cannot grant additional road movement or topology permissions. Use fresh locked source and independently reconstruct road lineage, then derive a complete affected ownership/dependency closure. No caller-provided authority or arbitrary imported city.

## Proposed risk-based sequence
A. Read-only inventory and ownership contract: identify existing block/face, parcel, building, frontage/access, district, defense, landscape, metadata, and provenance contracts; record gaps; design detached source/candidate/lineage evidence and one bounded operation root. Select small locked positive and negative fixtures, independently known ownership correspondence. Stop for ambiguity or schema/policy need.
B. Face correspondence: deterministic unchanged/reshaped/split/merged/new/deleted face classification; stable ID survival, fresh reservations, complete typed ledger, bounded affected closure; refuse unsupported ambiguous ownership or unapproved deletion.
C. Property reconciliation: preserve authored/unknown and unaffected records, ownership/access/frontage, non-overlap, envelope and setback; use nondestructive reshaping first, independent validation, no automatic frontage or demolition concession.
D. Building reconciliation: reuse qualified SRE packing/restoration where permitted; preserve metadata/identity; explicit permissions for any retirement/regeneration/scale; not new destruction authority.
E. Cross-domain verification: district, defense, gates, water/terrain, existing domain providers, exact road intent, full referential integrity, outside-scope byte equivalence, identity allocation and persistence round-trip. Independent reconstruction, negative controls, deterministic refusals.
F. Detached qualification: predeclared representative locked source/road candidates and independent expected outcomes; report road success versus full-city success separately, complete CI and phase checkpoint. No runtime publication.

A is proposed first after P5 exact full draft regression and separate approval. B–F are sequencing only; refine acceptance from actual contracts, stop for schema/authority changes and preserve independent verification. No live UI, merge, release, P7 or P8 approval. New destruction or broader road geometry cannot be inferred from phase progression. Proposed rollback: revert isolated implementation commit while retaining evidence; accepted city remains unchanged throughout detached work.
