# ARES landscape-aware spatial constraint architecture
Status: owner-approved planning amendment, 2026-11-26 Pacific. Documented on PR163 only; no implementation authorization, schema decision, new editing permission or release action. ARES remains paused for discussion and renewed explicit go-ahead after W4 closeout and exact-main qualification. This supplements [the approved ARES plan](ares-approved-plan.md); all existing SAFE-01–15 contracts and qualification thresholds remain binding.

## Objective and boundary
ARES must reason about the real city environment from P1, not treat terrain/water as later exceptions. Current 2D accepted Schema 11 city and existing authoritative validators remain the source of truth. Develop reusable read-only adapters for existing rivers, lakes, coast/ocean boundaries, terrain hills/mountains, crossings/bridges and fortification dependencies. Inspect actual model semantics and supported feature combinations before coding; distinguish actual elevation data from categorical 2D terrain. Where current schema cannot represent a feature, use detached synthetic contract tests, label that capability unsupported in production, and stop for separate schema/feature approval if required. Do not claim that new terrain authoring, height fields, bridges, harbors, tunnels, elevated roads or 3D editing are delivered by ARES. These remain future integrations. A road movement never grants water or terrain modification rights.

## ARES-L01–L10: mandatory extensibility design
- L01 — Common provider interface, versioned detached read-only DTOs and independently reproducible evidence. Define abstract contract during P1, validate against real accepted models during P2. Provider discovery must not itself mutate the city. Existing source modules (water-model, water-regions, water-geometry, water-shore-topology, river-editing, lake-geometry, terrain-model, terrain-geometry, road-graph and defense/water/terrain relationship validators) are investigation starting points, not permission to replace their rules.
- L02 — Stable typed feature/constraint references and provenance, geometry (2D regions, boundary segments, corridors, points), affected dependency/anchor refs, exact original source identity and revision, geometry/evidence fingerprint, hard physical/domain rule versus permission/protection versus soft optimization preference; express units and coordinate frame explicitly. Read-only visibility is distinct from writability. Unknown types, missing capabilities or stale references fail closed where safety depends on them.
- L03 — Distinguish land containment, road width and setback/clearance, protected infrastructure anchors, valid supported crossings and conditional access, future vertical clearance and slope. A feature may supply multiple constraints; all applicable hard constraints must hold. No blanket permission overrides, hidden road-intent clipping, or preference weighting that trades away hard safety.
- L04 — Spatial queries and conservative deterministic swept/final footprint discovery. Use indexed broad phase if useful, exact narrow phase under existing domain semantics; stable sort and deduplicate typed refs, account query and verification work within applicable budgets; cached derived index keyed by exact accepted snapshot/revision plus relevant geometry and rule version, invalidate on any dependency edit/adopt/Undo/Redo. No persisted second city representation. Distinguish sweep detection from rules requiring only valid final geometry; preserve existing swept protection rules where applicable.
- L05 — Water adapters for rivers and both banks, lake polygons and shorelines (including multiple independent lakes where represented), coast/ocean-facing land boundaries, bridge/crossing anchors and related water/defense references. No ordinary road through water or outside land; existing supported bridge crossings use domain validators, not generic intersection exemptions. River and coast coexistence, lake adjacency and mixed shore topology are explicit corpus strata. Existing crossing or bank geometry may not be relocated under ordinary road permissions.
- L06 — Terrain adapters for currently represented hills, mountains, rock/terrain exclusion regions and terrain-bound infrastructure. Provide explicit capability flags for slope/height/vertical datum availability, optional future elevation range and vertical-clearance query extension points. Missing elevations are unknown, never zero or assumed traversable; current valid 2D operations retain supported 2D rules without inventing slopes. A true 3D-dependent operation with absent required data is unsupported, not declared physically impossible. No schema bump or height inference from decorative rendering.
- L07 — Extensible crossing/structure semantics: future bridge relocation/new bridges, tunnels, viaducts, elevated roads, mountain passes, coastal engineering/harbors and landscape editing attach as explicit separately authorized capabilities and domain validators. A generic road split/merge or restructuring permission is not sufficient. Reuse typed constraint provider contract for future water/terrain editors and derived 3D; preserve accepted 2D authority and compatibility.
- L08 — Independent diagnostics and candidate verification across RTRE/SRE/block reconciliation. Distinguish verified hard-domain violation, protected/unauthorized feature, unsupported capability, ambiguous ownership/unknown data, budget exhaustion and verification failure; never infer global infeasibility from a bounded search. Return exact blocker references, constraint code/stage and reproducible evidence; no automatically destructive escalation. Neighboring road alternatives may follow bank/shore/terrain boundaries or detour only within authorized scope and exact selected road intent.
- L09 — Atomic review and publication. Bind constraint evidence/rule versions and relevant accepted dependencies to host session, revision, exact operation, candidate fingerprint and explicit permission; independent replay and current authoritative full-project validation before Apply; stale/canceled work cannot publish. Existing Undo/Redo, portable/workspace formats, offline worker, identity lineage and unchanged external record safeguards remain. Show actual blocker and applicable authority; no unverified landscape-change claims.
- L10 — Extensibility tests. Add a detached synthetic provider/constraint (including optional vertical constraint with explicit units/datum) without editing ARES search/coordinator core; verify deterministic enumeration, stale invalidation, separate capability/permission checks and fail-closed handling. Additional providers should register through the stable interface and use domain verifiers, not introduce new central per-feature branches. Version the detached contract; accepted schema remains unchanged unless separately approved.

## Qualification additions
Keep existing minimum 10,000 topology/recovery scenarios, reference-witness construction, recovery thresholds and complete regression coverage unchanged. Include landscape strata within the corpus; expand total if necessary rather than dropping existing strata. Predeclare counts and combinations before tests, separately label cheap preflight/contract probes versus costly full solves. Include dry controls; river banks with/without valid bridge, water-crossing attempt and legal approach movement; multiple lakes, narrow gaps and irregular lake shores; coast-only, river+coast, lake+coast and river+lake; mountain/hill/rock exclusion, permitted pass/route around terrain and terrain-bound defenses; combined river+lake+coast+terrain cases in supported current representations. If a combined feature fixture cannot be represented today, record exact limitation and test the combined contract in detached fixtures, not as a claimed live product capability. Cross landscape strata with quadrilateral/pentagon/branch/loop, dense and spacious, multiple city sizes and all morphologies, ±1/10/25/50/75/100 axis and diagonal offsets including independently feasible 100-unit references. Include near tangency, narrow clearance, protected bridge/gate anchors, no-crossing and unsupported crossing, mixed constraints, authored records, stale landscape edits during search/review, repeated Apply/Undo/Redo/save/reopen, and Firefox/Chromium offline and desktop/ultrawide representative paths.

Report attempted denominators, eligibility and recovery by landscape and movement strata; explicitly separate known feasible, known invalid reference, permission-limited, unsupported, and unresolved bounded-search cases. Synthetic future constraints must not inflate current product recovery rates. Require zero invalid accepted cities, zero unauthorized landscape/infrastructure/author-content changes, zero stale publications, 100% successful transactions reversible, no regressions to existing strict results and exact existing CI contracts. Future height/slope/tunnel/crossing features have their own later product acceptance and separate authorization.

## Implementation safeguards and tradeoffs
Complexity: high, particularly geometry at mixed shore/terrain boundaries and dependency reconciliation. Reuse existing validators and derive indices; do not rewrite water/terrain models or build a generalized 3D geometry engine in 0.3.0. Budget provider queries and measure deterministic scope/verification overhead on dry and mixed fixtures; no new root search budget or relaxed CI without approval. Prefer explicit capability gates and no-op adapters for absent optional features over pretending unsupported data is known. Surface blockers and stop for owner decision when schema, unsupported combined landscape model, protected infrastructure permissions or mandatory test policy would need change. No immediate user-visible changes.
