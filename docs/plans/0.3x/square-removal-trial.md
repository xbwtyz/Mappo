Detached layout validation omits only the nonexistent square from coverage; same graph and development validators. Report refusals, counts, protected roads/nodes, maximum degree, area and source fingerprints. Not persisted Schema 11 projects; no recovery claim. ## S2–S4 proposed sequencing
S2: qualify S1, audit square assumptions in road solver, face extraction, protected witness/independent audit and project validation. Design detached no-square adapter without changing production semantics or forging schema-valid data; if it cannot preserve independent safety contracts, report blocked rather than comparing unequal validators. Pilot exact matched movement selection rules, no tuning on final cohort. Match road group class, size, spatial stratum and offset rather than ID, retain selection/generation refusals and distinguish unmatched cases. Same search budget, displacement and permissions in each arm. S3: preregister/freeze 25 new recipe pairs per morphology, all generation/selection outcomes before recovery, 100 pairs / 200 cities, no replacement of failures. Same seeds, outline, center, size, movement offset (absolute each axis <=100), deterministic selection policy and independent verification; archive exact input and result provenance. S4: compare both all-attempt and boundary-compliant denominators, paired successes/disagreements, morphology-specific changes, structural refusal causes, work, junction degree, protected counts, visual and block usability review. Separate decision on whether square is mandatory, optional or ordinary editable land. Results do not establish whole-city land reconciliation; no B1 until decision.
