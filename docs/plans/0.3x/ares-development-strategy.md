## Execution and evidence
Use focused, reviewable PRs grouped by dependency and risk, fresh branches from verified main for substantial work, and separate explicit merge approvals plus exact-main full qualification. Follow DEV-OPT batching heuristics: 3–5 low-risk compatible tasks, 2–3 closely related medium-risk tasks, isolated high-risk geometry/topology/identity changes and separate approval for critical safety/schema/publishing policy. These are heuristics, not quotas. Hosted research precedes consequential strategy replacement, at matched inputs, permissions, work budgets and versions. Detached contracts and algorithms precede integrated engines, offline workers and mounted UI. Preserve the qualified W4 SRE path as a fallback, never silently change user mode/intent/authority or bypass verification; use only supported operations and explicit diagnostics. Preserve full coverage, 16 Node shards, four browser suites, routing and aggregate; heavy C7 benchmark remains opt-in. No duplicate local full CI. Record bounded approvals, exact source, tests, artifacts, rollback, blockers and next action in persistent checkpoints. Green is evidence, not authorization.

## Qualification and safety
Freeze fixture IDs/seeds, permissions, size strata, reference witness method, counts, held-out set, metrics and denominator rules before final evaluation. Independently feasible small/moderate/large movement recovery targets remain 99/95/90%, topology restructure 85%. At least 10,000 topology/recovery scenarios including supported mixed landscapes, all existing movement/shape/morphology/density strata, explicit feasible 100-unit cases, protected content and repeated edits. Distinguish preflight/contract probes from full solves; report all attempted cases, feasible and invalid references, unknown/unsupported, permission and scope limits, budget exhaustion, verification failures and outcome by stratum. Synthetic future capabilities do not inflate live results. No post-hoc exclusions or threshold relaxation; investigate misses and stop for owner decision if a contract must change. Zero invalid accepted cities, unauthorized data loss or stale publications; every successful transaction reversible within the supported history contract. Preserve existing strict and locked regressions and independently verify every candidate before Apply. Use representative manual checkpoints as functionality becomes available; final P9 owner acceptance remains mandatory.

Measure matched W4 baseline versus serial ARES and serial versus parallel at identical budgets; record success, preservation, work, time to first verified incumbent/final reviewed candidate, overhead, memory and responsiveness. Default parallel execution only after demonstrated benefit and parity; exact resource and performance limits are detailed research/design gates, not approved invented values. Developer documentation includes architecture, typed APIs, extension examples and reproducible troubleshooting. Correct in-scope defects and requalify, investigate missed recovery/performance, stop for any material architecture, schema, permission, safety, CI or regression-contract change. No implementation, merge, release, tag or Pages authorization inferred from this planning approval.
