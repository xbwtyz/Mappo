## Interaction and authority
Retain normal road selection, connected-group movement, numeric inputs, draft lifecycle and accepted-city isolation. Dragging uses lightweight preview; release or deliberate numeric commit may initiate a bounded inexpensive validation/adaptation path under visible mode, scope and existing permissions. Coalesce obsolete intents and never run expensive recovery continuously during pointer movement. No automatic escalation, demolition, regeneration or extra concessions; costly recovery requires explicit initiation. Automatic path budgets, scheduling/debounce, scope units/defaults and quality eligibility require measured detailed design, not new values inferred from the mockup. Adaptive is the approved future mode default; current W4 behavior unchanged. Preserve current Standard/High restrictions until approved research justifies change. Inspector exposes mode, quality, bounded scope, separate permissions; advanced profile and worker options only when qualified. Remember safe preferences where supported and approved; consequential permissions reset on new drafts, never silently persisted or inferred from previous consent. No new workspace/schema fields without separate approval.

## Candidate retention and selection
Plan a bounded, deterministic, independently verified candidate collection with up to three meaningfully different alternatives initially. Do not require three or delay availability of an existing verified solution merely to fill slots. Candidate diversity and ranking must reflect independently computed consequences and stable identities/lineage; collapse equivalent candidates, use stable tie-breaks, retain verified incumbent and enforce shared search budget and memory limits. Define diversity, eviction, resource limits and profile weights in detailed design and qualify before multi-solution activation. Profiles may change ranking, never hard validity/authority. A recommendation is based on the active profile and known verified candidates, not proof of global optimum. Show comparable typed counts/areas and exact changed identities: roads, intersections/topology, property/plot/building effects, removals, restored surviving IDs, additions and affected scope. Retention percentage requires explicit denominator; unavailable evidence must be labeled, not invented. Selected-lineage audit is not all attempted trials. Comparing candidates must not modify accepted city. Selecting a candidate binds exact evidence and fingerprint to review/consent; selection or intent/permission/revision changes invalidate prior Apply tokens and stale results. Review best while running stops at a safe logical checkpoint before independent review; Continue retains authorized budget/checkpoint. Fresh verification and atomic Apply/Undo/Redo/portable persistence remain mandatory.

## Map, progress and diagnostics
Reuse existing map/inspector and SRE lifecycle; provide Before, After and Changes views, highlight exact original/requested/adapted road geometry, changed and removed development, restored surviving and regenerated identities, known blockers and protected features where evidence supports them. Use distinct shapes/line styles/text and legends, not color alone. Provide equivalent accessible textual review and usable keyboard/numeric controls, focus and status announcements, compact desktop/ultrawide panels. No mandatory wizard or persistent oversized inspector; avoid gratuitous confirmations but preserve explicit Review/Apply. Shared presentation contracts may be reused for later domains without implementing those editors now.
Progress reflects bounded work consumed, phase and verified incumbent, not invented time remaining or certainty. Distinguish no solution within budget, policy/scope limits, unsupported capability, actual proven obstruction, invalid input, cancellation and verification failure. Highlight only grounded blocker identities and geometry. Offer actionable next steps within authority; no automatic permission expansion, hidden displacement reduction or claim of global infeasibility from search exhaustion. Preserve prior accepted state on refusal. Worker/candidate errors and memory caps produce honest diagnostics and safe fallback, not unverified success.
